# SAOR Claim Boundary v1.3.0

SAOR v1.3.0 proves the following within its stated transfer semantics and prime-context setting:

- exact dual-twin masking semantics;
- product twinhood under Prime Cover and Atom Deficit;
- exact reduction of one-sided sum separation to safe-filler representability;
- the Two-Filler Eventual Separator Lemma;
- effective finite critical-support synthesis;
- future stability of the synthesized signature;
- the Anchored-Cone Corollary: every distinct equal-sum/equal-product pair admits an explicit upward-closed cone of prime contexts on which the synthesis theorem applies;
- for the fixed two-module SAOR system, unique minimum critical support `{5,7,11}` among signatures of the form `(N,S,supp intersect C)` and the complete future classifier `K=(D4,D5,eps5,eps7,eps11)`;
- structural aggregate compression through `(D4,D5)` for observables depending only on `(N,S)`;
- a support-necessity extraction procedure whose positive necessity certificates are explicit and definition-level checked.

SAOR v1.3.0 does **not** claim:

- classification of contexts outside the anchored cone for an arbitrary base pair;
- a theorem for arbitrary composite-valued or mixed context alphabets;
- classification of arbitrary transfer-path reachability;
- minimality of the general synthesized critical set;
- that failure to find a support witness within a finite search bound proves non-necessity;
- novelty of equal-sum/equal-product realizations, graph twins/modules, the classical Frobenius/coin-representability problem, numerical-semigroup gap phenomena, contextual equivalence as an abstract concept, or affine integer characters;
- historical priority.

The repository uses generic comparison categories rather than a bibliography. No external theorem or article is used as mathematical input to the SAOR proofs in this package.

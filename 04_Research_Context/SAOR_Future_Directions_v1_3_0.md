# SAOR Future Directions v1.3.0

Open directions include:

- proving global non-necessity criteria for candidate critical-support coordinates;
- classifying contexts outside the anchored cone, where the product side can remain nontrivial;
- determining sharper or exact atom thresholds where the sufficient anchored threshold is not sharp;
- explaining the support-necessity patterns observed across anchored pairs;
- composite-valued or mixed context alphabets;
- synthesis across interacting origin modules;
- canonical minimization of future-stable signatures;
- formal mechanization of the general theorem and sharp derivation;
- integration with exact arithmetic and structural reasoning software.

These are research directions rather than claims of the present package.

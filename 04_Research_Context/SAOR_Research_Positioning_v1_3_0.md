# SAOR Research Positioning v1.3.0

SAOR is positioned as a structural arithmetic theory of realization behaviour under common context.

The central object is not a numerical value alone, but the distinguishability class of an arithmetic realization under exact retained-factor tests after compatible future context.

v1.3.0 contains two complementary levels:

1. a general synthesis theorem that converts admissible equal-projection base data into an exact finite masking classifier, together with an Anchored-Cone Corollary that places every distinct equal-sum/equal-product pair into that regime after an explicit finite common prime anchor;
2. a sharp two-module classification with unique minimum critical support (within `(N,S,supp intersect C)` signatures) and an iff future quotient.

The general result provides effective synthesis and universal anchored-cone reach; the sharp result provides exact unique-minimum support and constructive completeness for the fixed two-module system.

# Aggregate Compression Corollary Example

The aggregate rank-two coordinates reconstruct the context statistics exactly:

`N=D4-D5+7`

`S=5D4-4D5+31`.

Thus two contexts with equal `(D4,D5)` have equal `(N,S)`. The support-sensitive theorem shows why this is not sufficient for the full all-prime classifier: prime presence can change low-residual safe-filler availability even when `N` and `S` agree.

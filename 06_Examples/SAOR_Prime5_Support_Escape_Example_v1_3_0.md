# Prime-5 Support Escape Example

Take

`E=(3,7)`

and

`F=(5,5)`.

Both satisfy

`N=2`

`S=10`

and therefore

`(D4,D5)=(-1,4)`.

Yet

`joint_status(E)=(MASKED,UNMASKED)`

while

`joint_status(F)=(MASKED,MASKED)`.

Thus aggregate context statistics do not determine masking once support-sensitive contexts are admitted.
